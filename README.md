When Is Quantum Gravity Required?

A Finite Experimental Adjudication Framework

DOI: https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.22833457

This repository contains one coordinated Structural Flow publication package centered on the scientific paper When Is Quantum Gravity Required? A Finite Experimental Adjudication Framework.

The paper asks a deliberately narrower question than “what is quantum gravity?”:

Under what empirical conditions would no adequate classical gravitational description remain available at a declared physical scope, so that a quantum-gravity resolution becomes required there?

The work does not select a microscopic quantum-gravity theory.

Scientific criterion

The paper separates four requirements that must be established at one declared scope:

the relevant source/probe degrees of freedom independently support the quantum description used in the inference;

the quantum structure is load-bearing for the tested effect;

the effect is independently attributable to gravity;

the predeclared admitted classical gravitational comparison class fails to reproduce the complete registered data.

Only their conjunction supports the scoped conclusion that a quantum-gravity resolution is required.

Failure of one particular classical model is not enough.

Likewise, even successful exclusion of the admitted classical comparison class does not select gravitons, strings, loops, quantum geometry, discreteness, or any other microscopic ontology.

Formal disposition

The formal contract machine-checks the conditional logic of the adjudication.

It does not establish that any experimental premise has been satisfied.

A clean Lean elaboration means that the encoded definitions, theorem statements, proofs, promotion rules, blockers, and countermodels type-check together at their declared formal scopes.

It does not mean:

the proposed experiment has succeeded;

the admitted comparison class is empirically exhausted;

gravity has been shown to require quantization;

a particular microscopic QG theory is correct;

or the Universal Kernel is empirically validated.

Reopening rules

The scientific conclusion remains reopenable.

It reopens if, for example:

a new faithful classical gravitational representation reproduces the same complete registered data;

gravity attribution is defeated;

an alternate quantum-information route survives;

the finite quantum interface fails;

an admitted nonlocal, nonlinear, stochastic, hybrid, or other classical alternative survives;

the comparison-class coverage warrant fails;

or the experimental confidence construction or nuisance treatment is invalidated.

A scientific reopening does not automatically reopen the Universal Kernel.

The kernel reopens only if demonstrated pressure reaches genuinely universal machine structure rather than QG-specific premises or contract logic.
